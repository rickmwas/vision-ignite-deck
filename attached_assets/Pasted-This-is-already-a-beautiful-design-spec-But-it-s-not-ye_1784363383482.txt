The biggest mistake almost everyone makes is optimizing for beautiful slides instead of investor psychology.

YC partners have said repeatedly:

"We invest because we understand the founder's insight—not because the deck looks good."

Your current deck still follows a traditional startup sequence.

I'd redesign it around what I call the Curiosity Loop, where every slide creates a question that only the next slide answers.

YC-Level Design Philosophy

Replace:

Beautiful slides.

With:

Slides that force people to lean forward.

The audience should never think

"Next slide."

Instead they should think

"Wait... what?"

My biggest recommendation

Don't build a pitch deck.

Build a movie.

Think of it like a Pixar short:

Act 1: Make us care about Mama Akinyi.
Act 2: Reveal why the system failed her—not because people didn't care, but because no one shared the same story.
Act 3: Reveal TotoAfya as the invisible infrastructure that reconnects the journey.
Act 4: Show the future: a continent where every mother has one continuous, coordinated care journey.

If you achieve that, people won't remember your animations or typography—they'll remember the story, the insight, and why TotoAfya had to exist. That's the kind of pitch that stands out in front of investors, judges, and accelerator panels
